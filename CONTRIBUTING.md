# Contributions

Useful print reports are welcome: include the model/version, printer and nozzle, material, layer height, orientation, supports and any scaling or other changes. A photo of the result is especially helpful. Please remove private information from screenshots before sharing.

For a proposed change, explain the problem, include the editable source and updated export, and state what was checked digitally or printed physically. Keep new model submissions original or provide the source and permission for any reused material. Do not submit third-party characters or logos without appropriate rights.

Contributions must be offered under the existing license of the affected files: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for collection assets and model-specific source, MIT for reusable tooling in `tools/`. Do not include credentials, printer access details or machine-specific G-code.
