# Contributions

Useful print reports are welcome: include the model/version, printer and nozzle, material, layer height, orientation, supports and any scaling or other changes. A photo of the result is especially helpful. Please remove private information from screenshots before sharing.

For a proposed change, explain the problem, include editable source when available and an updated export, and state what was checked digitally or printed physically. The original models include source; the fan-art releases currently contain meshes only. A slicer preview or successful print-start receipt is not evidence of a completed physical print.

Identify reused artwork and its source accurately. Credit modeling and print preparation separately from the underlying character or logo, and do not claim ownership or official endorsement of third-party material. New original submissions belong in `models/`; fan-art submissions must be clearly identified with their own provenance and applicable terms.

Contributions must follow the existing terms of the affected files: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for original models, their source and collection documentation; the [personal printing terms](fan-art/TERMS.md) for our contributions in `fan-art/`; MIT for reusable tooling in `tools/`. Offer only rights you can grant. These scopes do not grant rights in third-party artwork or change prior licenses. Do not include credentials, printer access details or machine-specific G-code.
