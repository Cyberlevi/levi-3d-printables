# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-08

- Added the original Spotted Cat V3: black/white STL parts, single-colour STL, a geometry-only 3MF assembly, actual CAD previews and editable sources.
- Added the 140 mm ECO PLA print setup (104.08 g and 6 h 55 min 58 s slicer estimates), English/Hungarian model notes and digital validation.
- Updated the gallery and catalog. First cat print in progress at publication; successful physical completion remains unverified.
- Preserved all existing models, their licenses and the collection support information.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-07

- Added Eddie BACKSTAGE and Metallica wall-sign fan-art models, with black/white STL parts, geometry-only 3MF assemblies, CAD previews and print notes. These are mesh-only releases.
- Added separate personal, noncommercial self-printing terms covering only rights in our own fan-art contributions. Third-party character and logo rights are not granted or claimed.
- Put the two fan-art signs first in the gallery and preserved the original models' CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license and editable sources.
- Added an optional Bitcoin support address and matching QR code to the English and Hungarian guides.

Experimental release. Successful physical prints remain unverified for all four models.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-07

First experimental public collection: BACKSTAGE lightning pick sign and long-neck dinosaur keyring. Includes STL parts, geometry-only 3MF, CAD previews, editable sources, English and Hungarian documentation, and digital validation reports.

Physical print status: unverified for both models.
